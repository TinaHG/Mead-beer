Let´s mead

The occasions can be many – a celebration, a thank you or just a thought for someone who deserves something special. That is why we hope that this mead can help create a small moment of presence and good taste experiences.

rewed with inspiration from Nordic traditions and nature’s own raw materials, this mead is our tribute to good craftsmanship and to the moments shared with others.

The tast of Idun mead

Our mead is inspired by this Nordic touch and is based on raw materials from the Nordic countryside. It is created as a refreshing tribute to the old traditions, where the sweetness of honey and the flavors of nature meet in a simple but characterful brew.

Apple & birch sap

A light and fresh mead with soft notes of apple and a touch of birch sap from the Nordic nature. The taste is mild, slightly sour and balanced with the natural sweetness of honey. A refreshing variant that gives a fresh expression when small moments are to be enjoyed

Lavender & rosehip

This mead has a gentle and aromatic taste, where the floral notes of lavender meet the light, fruity character of rosehip. Together they create an elegant and well-balanced drink with a touch of flowers and Nordic summer.

Plum - Alcohol 0.5%

A round and fruity mead with rich blue plum flavours. It has a mild sweetness and a soft character, which makes for a pleasant and light drink with a low alcohol percentage that still retains the mead's natural depth and golden hue.

In Norse mythology, Idun’s ælber gives the gods eternal life. This mead is inspired by a Nordic ice cream with ingredients from Norse nature. A fascinating tribute to the fine craftsmanship that Vikings were really good at.
